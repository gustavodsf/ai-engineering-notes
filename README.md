# AI Engineering Notes

Study material behind my LinkedIn posts. One folder per post, named after the post; inside, the post draft, its infographic, and one file per source, named after the source.

## Posts

1. [You're probably still overprompting Claude](./You're%20probably%20still%20overprompting%20Claude/) – Three habits for newer Claude models: don't spawn a subagent for every task, audit old instructions with `/doctor prompt-audit`, and use `/goal` so Claude doesn't stop early.
2. [No AI slop, a manifesto for better work](./No%20AI%20slop%2C%20a%20manifesto%20for%20better%20work/) – What "AI slop" is, and three habits to avoid it: review what matters, put real checks before the human, and measure what shipped.
3. [Clean code, but nobody dares to change it](./Clean%20code%2C%20but%20nobody%20dares%20to%20change%20it/) – Technical, cognitive and intent debt are three different problems; AI can pay the first while growing the other two, so pay the why first, then the understanding, then the code.
